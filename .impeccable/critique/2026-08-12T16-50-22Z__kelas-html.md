## Priority Issues

**[P0] Zoom unlock never re-checks after page load — confirmed via direct code read.** `tick()` in `kelas.js` (line 256-263) only updates the countdown *text* when the timer reaches zero; it never calls `applyZoomLock()` again. A student who loads the page while still locked, then leaves the tab open exactly as the tile instructs ("simpan halaman ini"), will see "Terkunci" forever past the real unlock moment, until they manually reload. This breaks the page's core promise at the exact moment it matters most (joining live class).
Fix: call `applyZoomLock({unlocked:true})` (or re-fetch status) inside `tick()` when `diffMs <= 0`.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P0] Denied/error states have no in-page recovery, only an off-site WhatsApp escape hatch.** `kelas.js` already implements a "switch account" flow (`disableAutoSelect()` + `showState('signin')`) for the functional-zone sign-out button — the same 3 lines are simply not reused on the denied/error panels, even though "wrong cached Google account" is a common, self-fixable cause of "denied."
Fix: add a "Coba akun lain" button to both panels reusing the existing sign-out handler.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Locked links likely drop out of the accessibility tree entirely.** Confirmed in code: `applyZoomLock`/`applyPracticeLock` both call `link.removeAttribute('href')` while keeping `aria-disabled="true"`. An `<a>` without `href` typically loses link semantics/focusability in most browsers — a locked quiz becomes invisible information for keyboard/screen-reader students, not merely dimmed.
Fix: keep the control focusable (real `<button disabled>`, or `tabindex="0"` + `role="link"`) so the unlock-date note stays discoverable non-visually.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P2] Schedule list is uncapped.** `api/verify-access.js` sends every future session with no cap; day 1 of a 10-day bootcamp could render 8-9 items at once in one card.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P3] Denied and error states are visually indistinguishable at a glance** — same white-on-black h1, same WhatsApp CTA, differentiated only by reading two paragraphs of prose.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (anxious first-timer)**: Google One Tap auto-suggests a previously-used cached account before Jordan even reads the "pastikan pakai email yang sama" hint; if they proceed with the wrong account, they land on "denied" with no in-page way to try again.

**Sam (screen reader/keyboard-only)**: Locked quiz/Zoom links likely vanish from the accessibility tree instead of announcing as disabled — Sam gets *less* information than a sighted user, the opposite of what `aria-disabled` is meant to provide.

**Casey (mobile, 2 minutes before class)**: The Zoom tile's own copy encourages leaving the tab open, but the join link can stay stuck on "Terkunci" past the real unlock moment because nothing re-checks client-side — exactly the scenario this persona lives in, and now confirmed as a real bug, not a hypothetical.
