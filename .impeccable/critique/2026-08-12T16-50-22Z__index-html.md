## Design Specificity Verdict
Mostly grounded (mentor score counters, EF EPI gap-chart with real citations, disabled-until-real testimonials are load-bearing and non-generic), but the page skeleton is standard landing-page anatomy and the hero's photo-less initials avatar row is a stock trust trope that sits oddly next to the page's actual differentiator (verified, checkable proof).

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` clean, 0 findings.

**Live evidence** (verified independently, not just relayed): heading structure clean (1 h1, no skips), all images have alt text, no horizontal overflow at 375px or 1440px, mobile drawer nav links measure 38-42px tall (under 44px), logo link 88x25 (fails height). One Assessment-B claim (nav text ~1:1 contrast across ~40% of scroll range) was investigated and found to be a **false positive**: caused by this session's sandboxed browser tab reporting `document.hidden = true` / non-compositing, which breaks scroll-event-based tests entirely (confirmed: `window.scrollTo()` did not move `scrollY` at all in this tab). Code review of the `is-stuck` mechanism (`script.js`, scrollY > 24 toggles a solid/blurred background) shows no logical defect.

## Overall Impression
A disciplined, purpose-built page let down by one live content bug and a handful of trust-continuity gaps at exactly the moments that matter most (proof going quiet before the CTA, counters that can silently show "0").
